UI Kit Forge

"Kit + style contract" writes four files: the atlas, its manifest, tokens.json and STYLE.md (roles, tokens, invariants — the input for agent-driven development). The manifest is TexturePacker JSON-hash plus what a slicer needs: border/cap (nine-slice, straight into TiledSprite2D.sliceBorder*), body/midY (the opaque body, for centring a caption), anchors (where the stripped captions belonged) and warnings. Every PNG is drawn without its caption — the engine draws that.
